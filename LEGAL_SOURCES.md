# External legal references used by the HR practice game

These sources are educational references, not legal advice. The game UI tells users to verify current Ministry of Labour, SSB, IRD or qualified counsel guidance because Myanmar requirements vary by sector, coverage, contract, notification and effective date.

- ILO Guide to the Myanmar Labour Law: https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/Hachemian.Sara.C%40dol.gov/ILOGUI~1.PDF
  - Covers employment-contract terms, probation, leave, termination, severance, labour disputes and workplace grievance paths.
- ILO Myanmar labour laws and COVID-19 FAQ: https://tradefordecentwork.ilo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Myanmar-Labour-Laws-and-COVID-19-FAQ.pdf
  - Covers public holidays, earned leave, sick leave, minimum wage and wage payment timing, with scope/date caveats.
- 2026 Myanmar Employment Guide (secondary current guide): https://www.sailglobal.com/global-employment/2026-myanmar-employment-guide-en?continent=Asia&page=3
  - Used only as a current-date cross-check for wage, leave, SSB and termination topics; not presented as an official government source.
